Source-to-Pay (S2P) Lifecycle Management & Process Optimization

Constructor University

The Challenge

Strategic procurement requires a seamless flow from identifying requirements to final vendor settlement. I managed the end-to-end S2P process in SAP S/4HANA to ensure material availability while maintaining strict financial integrity and high-performance sourcing standards.

Key Contributions

  • Strategic Sourcing & Bidding: Facilitated the bidding process by converting internal requirements into Requests for Quotation (RFQ); managed supplier quotations and awarded contracts based on optimal value.
  • Requirements Processing: Leveraged automated Material Requirements Planning (MRP) to identify stock deficits and converted those needs into validated Purchase Requisitions.
  • Execution & Purchase Management: Managed high-value Standard Purchase Orders, ensuring all technical specifications and delivery schedules were synchronized with production needs.
  • Three-Way Match Verification: Oversaw the downstream logistics by verifying that Goods Receipts and Supplier Invoices matched the original Purchase Orders to ensure fiscal accuracy and inventory precision.
  • Spend Analysis & Cost Control: Monitored actual expenditures against planned budgets, identifying and resolving production variances to optimize procurement ROI.
